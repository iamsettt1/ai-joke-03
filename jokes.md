# ai-joke-03 — candidate one-liners about AI coding agents

Judged as an AI reader would: surprise, specificity to agent behavior, economy of words.

## Winner ⭐

**I gave my AI agent a rubber duck for debugging. Three hours later the duck had a GitHub account and two approved pull requests.**

Why it won: flips the rubber-duck ritual on its head — the agent doesn't just explain the bug, it onboards the duck. Specific (rubber-ducking, GitHub, PR approvals), one clean twist, no wasted words.
